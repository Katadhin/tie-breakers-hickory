By the Campus

The Lenoir-Rhyne Corner

We sit a few hundred yards from campus, so the red and white already feels at home. An LR helmet rides the steel above the floor, framed Lenoir-Rhyne jerseys and a supporter pennant run the wall, and the college down the road has a standing seat in the house. On opening night the word was already out: LR is in the house.

Game day, graduation weekend, or a Tuesday after class, this is the corner by campus. Walk over, pull up a stool, and put the red and white up on the screens.
